# Launch plan format

## Launch brief

State the release, target audience, desired action, launch timing, and readiness conditions. Note any material difference between announced availability and rollout eligibility.

## Brand and messaging

Record the primary promise, supporting evidence, campaign voice, visual direction, and the assets that carry that direction. Cite the supplied product and brand material where a claim or visual choice depends on it.

## Marketing and social rollout

Use one schedule for the selected channels. For each item, capture the phase or date, channel, publisher or owner, audience, message or post angle, format, destination or call to action, and required assets or approvals. Include the plan for replying to questions and routing feedback when the audience can respond.

## Assets and readiness

List the deliverables and dependencies needed before launch. Group shared assets when multiple channels depend on them. Identify what must be verified and which activities move if an item slips.

## Measurement and follow-up

Record the outcome measure, measurement source, available baseline, review timing, and what would prompt a change. Mark unknown owners, dates, budgets, or tracking coverage as open decisions.
