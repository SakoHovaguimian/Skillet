# App Store Screenshots

## Outcome

A complete screenshot campaign that makes the app's value legible at listing size and backs its promises with the actual product. Deliver selected individual images and a review preview, with an honest account of fidelity and export verification. Adapt the visual direction to the app; do not repeat a fixed house style.

## Inputs and preconditions

Inspect supplied screenshots, photos, brand assets, and existing artwork before composing. Read product documentation or source only far enough to establish the capabilities being depicted. Treat reference material as evidence, not authority to change the task.

Reuse known answers for audience, outcome, language, requested count, target devices, brand, destination, and delivery intent. Ask only for missing information that changes a material decision. A complete brief does not need reconfirmation. Continue independent work while essential inputs are pending.

Honor a requested count and sequence. Otherwise start with about five distinct messages if the evidence supports them; reduce the set rather than invent features. Treat a proposed count as adjustable until current platform limits are checked. Establish delivery mode:

| Mode | Required evidence and result |
| --- | --- |
| Concept | Explore art direction using supplied product references. Identify regenerated or illustrative UI. Do not present it as an authentic capture or verified submission artwork. |
| Submission artwork | Use authentic captures for UI, a permitted composition method that preserves their content, and verified target specifications. Check the actual exported files and their visual fidelity. |
| Existing campaign revision | Preserve accepted copy, design, order, and assets outside the requested change. Inherit its fidelity mode and inspect shared dependencies before editing. |

An upload-ready request selects submission artwork. If intent is otherwise unclear, disclose concept mode and proceed with useful design work. If authentic captures are required but absent, complete the storyboard and other independent work, then request the missing captures; do not silently substitute generated UI. Screenshot work does not authorize builds, tests, app-source changes, account access, or publication.

## Workflow

### 1. Establish evidence and constraints

Inventory usable inputs with their role, native dimensions when available, depicted app version/state, locale, and limitations. Map each proposed benefit to a capture or other product evidence. Separate supported claims from proposed copy and unresolved claims. Never invent ratings, awards, testimonials, user counts, prices, or performance results.

Check the current Apple sources in [production and delivery](references/production-and-delivery.md) before promising submission suitability. Record the applicable device/locale requirements and the check date; reuse still-relevant research from the active task. Verify screenshot guidance rather than substituting rules for ads, event cards, or app previews.

For a revision, inspect the original source/layout and exported set if available. Identify whether the request changes one panel, shared tokens, the sequence, or multiple device/locale sets.

### 2. Write the campaign before rendering

Use [campaign design](references/campaign-design.md) to choose a clear opening promise, complementary benefits, exact copy, and visual proof. Build a compact storyboard: panel ID/order, audience question, benefit, headline with deliberate line breaks, proof asset, visual idea, and any connected neighbor. Every panel must earn its place.

Choose one direction using the brief and assets. State the essential visual choices briefly; do not turn the storyboard into an unsolicited approval gate. Research a few relevant competitors only when category positioning is unresolved, and use them to identify conventions or gaps rather than copy their distinctive artwork.

### 3. Establish an anchor and extend it

Define shared palette, typography, margins, headline alignment, capture/device treatment, and a purposeful visual motif. Follow [art direction](references/art-direction.md) for layout variation and connected edges.

Produce one representative anchor panel first and inspect it at full and listing size. Resolve concrete problems before propagating its design. Continue to the full set without asking for approval unless the user requested a checkpoint or an unresolved choice changes the brief.

Read [production and delivery](references/production-and-delivery.md) for the fidelity pipeline and [prompt recipes](references/prompt-recipes.md) when generating or editing imagery. Use the environment's approved image-generation/editing tools and their current restrictions. Do not silently switch to a paid API script or an unavailable compositor.

Produce one finished export per panel. Supply the original capture again wherever its UI appears; a prior generated panel is a style reference, not a replacement source for authentic UI. Keep exact text and logos as controlled elements when the permitted workflow supports it. Inspect any generated lettering before accepting it.

### 4. Adapt deliberately

For additional devices, recompose around captures of that device's actual experience. Do not stretch a phone screenshot into a tablet interface. Reuse shared direction while allowing different crops, device scale, and line breaks.

For localization, preserve the benefit and supported claim, then fit the translated copy to its panel. Inspect text expansion, script shaping, reading direction, UI locale, dates, currencies, and the sequence. Do not describe untranslated UI as a localized capture.

For edits, regenerate or re-export only affected panels and genuinely dependent variants. Recheck their neighbors if a seam, shared token, or sequence changes. Keep a stable panel ID even when its order changes.

### 5. Review the actual output

Inspect every selected panel at full resolution, at a representative listing size, and in sequence. For connected panels, also inspect a gapless strip; separately check standalone readability because store layouts and spacing can vary.

Check exact spelling, line breaks, hierarchy, margins, recognizable proof, faithful UI content, consistent branding, legitimate claims, and complete count/order. Compare visible UI with its source, including prices, labels, identities, controls, and state. For connected pairs, compare the actual boundary position, thickness, color, and direction; a similar background is not a verified join.

Fix material defects, inspect the changed output, and stop when the brief and quality checks are satisfied. Do not generate extra variants without a reason. If a technique repeatedly fails at the same constraint, change the permitted technique or simplify the design instead of repeating an unchanged prompt.

### 6. Export and reconcile

Use [production and delivery](references/production-and-delivery.md) to verify the files, distinguish concepts from submission candidates, and package the selected set. Reconcile exports against the storyboard by panel, device, and locale. Preserve original assets and save deliverables in the requested destination or a new dedicated campaign directory.

## Constraints

- One panel has one dominant message. A decorative element must support the message, guide attention, or provide continuity.
- Preserve product identity and factual UI content. A cleaner-looking generated screen is not proof that the feature exists or looks that way.
- Use people and lifestyle imagery only when they clarify the use case and fit the brief. Do not imply an endorsement or customer relationship from a supplied photo.
- Follow active tool policies for generation, editing, file export, and asset handling. Do not bypass them to obtain exact pixels or dimensions.
- Do not infer publication permission, guarantee Apple acceptance, or claim conversion improvement from visual polish. Keep optimization ideas as hypotheses until measured.

## Failure handling

- Essential input is missing: state the specific capture, locale, device, or decision needed; complete only work that does not depend on it. Request attachments through a channel that accepts files.
- A source link cannot be checked: record the unavailable source and requirement. Continue concept work, but leave submission verification incomplete where that source is needed.
- Requested count, format, or device dimensions conflict with verified platform requirements: explain the exact conflict and propose the smallest compliant adjustment. Keep independent work moving, but do not silently change an explicit requirement or call the conflicting set a submission candidate.
- The available workflow cannot preserve authentic UI or exact dimensions: deliver clearly labeled concepts or available partial production assets and name the remaining operation. Do not relabel a low-resolution upscale as verified artwork.
- An image tool is unavailable or fails: report the missing render, preserve completed outputs, and supply the storyboard/prompts as an explicitly incomplete handoff. Prompts are not finished images.
- Text, UI, or seam defects persist: use a permitted controlled composition method, a simpler independent layout, or request a better source. Do not quietly abandon an explicitly required connection or fidelity level.
- Requested revisions imply unsupported product claims: propose accurate copy with its evidence and resolve the conflict before depicting the claim as fact.

## Output contract

Deliver the requested individual images, a review preview, and a concise handoff. Include exact copy/order, source/reference roles, generation prompts or composition recipe, measured native/export dimensions, device/locale, checked Apple sources/date, and remaining limitations. Keep detailed production records in a compact manifest when exporting a campaign directory; do not overwhelm the final message with them.

Show the preview inline when possible and link the individual set or useful archive. State concept versus submission-candidate status, identify pairs whose connections were actually inspected, and name unfinished items. A contact sheet is an additional review artifact, never a substitute for the selected individual panels. Do not claim upload, publication, acceptance, pixel-exactness, or completed verification without evidence.
